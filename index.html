Our story

Desi Roots is a neighbourhood restaurant built around everyday Indian meals. Our menu draws on South Indian tiffins, Hyderabadi-style biryani and home-style regional curries.

We cook in small batches, grind our own masalas and keep the menu simple, so you get honest food at a fair price. [Owner: add your own founding story and family details here.]
